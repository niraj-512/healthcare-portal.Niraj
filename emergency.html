Emergency Guide

Quick guidance for common medical emergencies

⚠️ Important

This information is for emergency guidance only. It does not replace professional medical care. In a serious emergency, contact emergency medical services or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

🫀

1. Heart Emergency

Warning Signs

  • Severe or persistent chest pain
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Fainting or severe weakness
  • Sudden sweating with discomfort

What to Do

Seek emergency medical help immediately. Keep the person calm and do not leave them alone.

🎗️

2. Cancer Emergency

Warning Signs

  • Severe breathing difficulty
  • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe confusion

What to Do

Get immediate medical attention and inform the patient's cancer treatment team.

🌡️

3. Diabetes / Sugar

Warning Signs

  • Severe confusion
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Seizure
  • Unable to safely swallow

What to Do

Get emergency medical help. If the person is unconscious or cannot swallow safely, do not give food or drink by mouth.

🧠

4. Neurological

Warning Signs

  • Sudden weakness or numbness
  • Difficulty speaking
  • Seizure
  • Loss of consciousness

What to Do

Seek immediate medical help. Note when the symptoms started and keep the person safe.

🫁

5. Respiratory

Warning Signs

  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Very fast or difficult breathing
  • Blue or grey lips
  • Loss of consciousness

What to Do

Seek emergency medical help immediately. Keep the person in a comfortable position while waiting for help.

🫘

6. Kidney

Warning Signs

  • Severe side or back pain
  • Very little or no urine
  • Blood in urine
  • Severe weakness or confusion

What to Do

Seek urgent medical attention, especially if severe pain or major changes in urination occur.

🩸

7. Blood

Warning Signs

  • Heavy bleeding
  • Fainting
  • Severe weakness
  • Breathing difficulty

What to Do

Get urgent medical care. Do not ignore heavy bleeding, fainting or severe weakness.

🦴

8. Bone / Injury

Warning Signs

  • Severe injury
  • Suspected fracture
  • Severe pain
  • Unable to move the injured part

What to Do

Get medical attention and avoid unnecessary movement of the injured body part.

👩‍⚕️

9. Women's Health

Warning Signs

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Severe abdominal or pelvic pain
  • Fainting or unconsciousness
  • Severe breathing difficulty

What to Do

Seek immediate medical care. During pregnancy, serious symptoms should be assessed urgently by a healthcare professional.

🚨 Serious Emergency?

Don't wait for the website. Contact emergency medical services or reach the nearest hospital immediately.
